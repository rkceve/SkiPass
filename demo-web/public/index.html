Sowbank

Borrow seeds in spring. Bring some back in autumn.

Sowbank is a free seed library run by gardeners on Alder Street. A borrower card lets you reserve packets from this season’s shelf.

Get a borrower card

We’ll send a 6-digit code to confirm it’s your address. There’s no password.

How borrowing works

  1. Reserve up to five packets from the shelf list.
  2. Pick them up at the Alder Street library during any open hour.
  3. Save seed from your strongest plants and bring back a packet by October.
